# Getting started and contact

Filed announcement text.

## Getting started

If you’ve never taped out a chip before, the Tiny Tapeout documentation walks through the process end to end, and the tools are all free and open source. Start by getting a UART transmitter out of a pin. Then make it programmable.

## Sign-up

If you’re interested, please fill out our sign-up form. We’ll send updates about the tapeout template, deadlines, as well as providing the final submission link. Note that filling out the form is not a commitment to participating, it’s just to receive updates!

We’ll add a final submission form to this page closer to the deadline!

If you have questions along the way, reach out to asic-competition@janestreet.com.
